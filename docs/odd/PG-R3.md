## 4. ODD items to confirm with the AV developer and operator

- [ ] Maximum operating speed per segment, and behaviour where the posted limit is 60 km/h or more
- [ ] Traffic-light recognition at each signal-controlled site, day and in heavy rain
- [ ] Yielding at crossings without signals, including late and partly hidden pedestrians
- [ ] School-zone hours and speed; schedule against school arrival and dismissal
- [ ] Lane changes and merges on multi-lane roads
- [ ] Interaction with public buses at shared stops
- [ ] Which service roads are inside the approved route, and a geofence for the rest
- [ ] Signal control disputed between OSM and LTA's traffic-light layer at 1 turn(s): right turn at 1.40424,103.89122 (osm only). Check on site.
- [ ] Weather limits: rainfall intensity at which service is suspended, and the data source used to decide (for example NEA rainfall readings)
- [ ] Lighting: service within daylight hours only, or night operation evidence
- [ ] Minimal risk manoeuvre: where on each segment the vehicle can stop safely
- [ ] Transitions between autonomous and manual driving: procedure and training (reviewed by LTA and an operator after the 17 Jan 2026 incident; see SOURCES.md)
- [ ] Remote monitoring: latency and coverage along the full route
